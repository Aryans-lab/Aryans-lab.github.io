Electrical, gain & radiation characterization of 4H-SiC LGADs

Silvaco Victory Device · Lucky-Drift impact ionization · C-DLTS

α_h(E) = a_h·exp(−b_h/E)  ·  a_h = 1.92×10⁷ cm⁻¹, b_h = 9.80×10⁶ V/cm  ·  V_pt = 75.5 V
  • Derived a first-principles Lucky-Drift impact-ionization model for 4H-SiC along ⟨0001⟩ and implemented it in Silvaco Victory Device.
  • Validated static C–V, 1/C² Mott–Schottky behaviour and reverse I–V on 50 µm epitaxial SICAR prototypes, with peak fields of 1.65–1.74 MV/cm.
  • Reproduced ²⁴¹Am α-particle charge collection and gain (M = 2.02–2.84 over 150–350 V) within 1σ, and quantified MIP internal gain at 2.06–2.81.
  • Modelled proton-irradiated C-DLTS trap compensation (Z1/2, EH3, EH6/7 up to 10¹⁴ neq/cm²), capturing Fermi-level pinning and α-CCE degradation to 52.5%.

Research project · Prof. Subhasish Basak Verified to 10⁻⁹

Shock waves & Burgers turbulence in 1D viscous fluids

Five solvers from scratch · exact Cole–Hopf reference

∂tu + u∂xu = ν∂xxu  ·  δ ∝ ν⁰·⁹⁸⁵  ·  E(k) ∝ k⁻²·⁰³
  • Implemented five 1D viscous Burgers solvers from scratch: Strang-split spectral FFT, Crank–Nicolson with Sherman–Morrison, Lax–Wendroff, Upwind and FTCS.
  • Verified spatial convergence (to spectral 10⁻⁹) and von Neumann stability against an 8,192-point exact Cole–Hopf reference.
  • Extracted shock thickness, the energy spectrum and bifractal structure-function scaling ζp.
  • Quantified upwind numerical viscosity (νnum/ν = 3.7 at N = 256) and grid-locking across three decades of ν.

Independent research project Cramér–Rao accuracy

Inverse PINNs & Cramér–Rao bound analysis for heat diffusion

Exact-constraint PINNs · 1D–3D · 4,000 pseudo-experiments

rel. L² = 6.0×10⁻⁴  ·  Var(θ̂) ≥ I(θ)⁻¹  ·  one-loop UV correction
  • Solved 1D–3D heat diffusion with exact-constraint PINNs and Levenberg–Marquardt optimisation, reaching 6.0×10⁻⁴ relative L² error.
  • Derived the closed-form ideal-PINN objective through a stochastic heat-equation equivalence, and proved that omitting the marginal log-determinant induces estimator bias.
  • Characterised UV variance scaling across 1D–3D and implemented a one-loop correction recovering Cramér–Rao bound accuracy across 4,000 pseudo-experiments.

03 — At the bench

Experimental physics & instrumentation.

Two semesters of NISER SPS laboratory work: recovering microvolt signals from noise, pulling the Landé g-factor out of an ESR line, and counting Franck–Hertz thresholds in neon.

Modern physics · spin resonance

RF electron spin resonance on DPPH

Recovered the resonance line of a DPPH sample with a phase-sensitive lock-in, then fitted the line to extract the Landé g-factor and the linewidth.

Measuredg ≈ 2.0036

Atomic & molecular spectroscopy

Hydrogen Balmer emission, iodine vapour rovibronic absorption, and the quantized inelastic thresholds of neon in a Franck–Hertz tube.

Optics · interferometry

Michelson & Fabry–Pérot interferometry

Aligned both instruments to measure laser coherence length, the free spectral range FSR = c/2nL, and cavity finesse — then profiled TEM₀₀ Gaussian beam propagation (w₀, zR), polarization states under Malus's law, and grating / slit diffraction.

Magnetism, OPAMPs and digital logic

Traced ferromagnetic B–H hysteresis loops and paramagnetic susceptibility by Quincke's method, captured Fizeau and Cornu fringes, then designed and tested IC 741 active filters, BJT amplifiers and TTL/CMOS counters.
